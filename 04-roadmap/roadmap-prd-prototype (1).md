- **Guardrail metric (M3), what must not drop or break:** Agreement rate: the share of journey starters who end in an agreement.  Baseline: 46.0% overall (3,846 of 8,360). Pilot segment baseline: 41.6% for variable-income and shift-work starters (2,862). Source: data-pack.md, Baseline rates and User split. What it is for  It is the metric that must not drop. The initiative adds explanation and a clearer question to a journey that already converts 46.0% of starters. If the new steps push people out, this is where it shows first, and it counterbalances the primary metric. Continuation at the income step could rise because people click through, and the agreement rate would catch it if they later abandon.  Suggested floor (my assumption, illustrative) Pilot segment: about 40.6%, one point below its baseline. Everyone else: 46.0%. Rule: Stop or review if either is breached for two consecutive weeks.
