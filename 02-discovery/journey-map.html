Future-state journey: Ana

Strategy: transparency and trust for vulnerable users

Role
Shift worker with variable income and two open claims, on her phone between shifts.
Goal
Set up an instalment plan she can keep, so she isn't caught out by a missed payment.
Friction
Unclear whether "income" means this month or a normal month (N-01); plan chosen by fewest months, then a missed payment (N-02).
1On her break
2Same session
3Same session
4Before first due date

  1. 1. Orient

    User action

    Reads why income is asked and kept → knows what she agrees to.

    Internal state

    Wary and short on time → reassured, continues instead of closing the tab.

    Pain point addressed

    No help text, fear of data use → trust before data entry.T-206, N-06

  2. 2. Describe

    User action

    Picks "typical month" and an income range → answers without fear of a wrong number.

    Internal state

    Unsure what to enter → in control, with skip option and saved progress.

    Pain point addressed

    Ambiguous income question, answers lost on back button → no restart.N-01, T-204

  3. 3. Choose

    User action

    Compares only plans that fit her pay day → stops choosing by fewest months.

    Internal state

    Anxious about cost → confident, having seen a missed-payment outcome beforehand.

    Pain point addressed

    Plan chosen by length, no consequences shown → informed choice.N-02, N-05, T-208

  4. 4. Commit

    User action

    Confirms plan, receives reminder before due date → first payment lands on time.

    Internal state

    Settled but watchful → supported; an agent can see her entries if she calls.

    Pain point addressed

    Late reminders, agents blind to entries → no repeating herself.T-207, T-205, N-08

Three advantages over the manual workaround

  1. Fitted answerReplaces payslip-and-calculator maths with ranged answers → fitted plan in minutes, not a closed tab.
  2. Consequences firstShows missed-payment consequences before confirming → she learns the cost before, not after, a broken plan.
  3. ContinuityKeeps her entries and progress → no restarting online or repeating herself on the phone.

Internal states are design intentions, not observed outcomes. One plan across several claims (B1, T-202) is out of scope. Stage 4 depends on fixing T-207.
